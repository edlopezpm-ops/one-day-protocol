# Understanding Your Mind

Part of [[Key Psychological Principles]].

- **Behaviour is goal-directed** — Nothing you do is random. Every pattern is aimed at something (teleology).
- **Some of those goals work against you** — You can pursue a goal for years without ever having chosen it.
- **Change the goal, not just the schedule** — New perspective produces new goals, which produce new behaviour. In that order.
- **Identity defends itself** — Your sense of self reacts to contradiction the way your body reacts to infection.
