# SOP 03 — Run the day

The map is theory. This is the part that does something.

**You need:** one day you can actually protect. A day off, a weekend day, anything where nobody needs you for a few hours. Not a Tuesday you squeeze it into.

---

## The night before

1. Open the **Workbook** tab.
2. Scroll to *Part 2 — Throughout the Day*, pick tomorrow's date, click **Download reminders (.ics)**.
3. Open the downloaded file. Your calendar app takes it and creates six alarms.
   - *Phone:* email the file to yourself and open it on the phone, or drop it in your cloud drive and tap it there. The alarms need to fire on the device that's with you.
   - *No calendar app?* Set six phone alarms manually at 11:00, 13:30, 15:15, 17:00, 19:30, 21:00 and label each one with its question from the Workbook.
4. Decide where you'll write: the browser Workbook, `Workbook.md` in Obsidian, or paper. Any is fine. Pick one and stay there.

---

## Morning — Psychological Excavation (15–30 min)

Do this **before** your phone, email, or anyone else's opinion reaches you.

1. **Anti-vision, 8 questions.** Write the true answer, not the presentable one. If an answer makes you want to stop writing, that's the one to keep going on.
2. **Vision, 3 questions.** Concrete detail beats inspiration. "An ordinary Tuesday" means what time you wake up, what's in front of you, who's there.

Rules that make this work:

- Write in full sentences, not bullets. Bullets let you skip the hard part.
- No editing while writing. No audience.
- If you're stuck on one, write "I don't want to answer this because…" and keep going. That's usually the answer.

---

## Through the day — Pattern Interruption

Six alarms. When one fires: **stop, read the question, answer it out loud or in one line.** Sixty seconds. Then go back to what you were doing.

| Time | Question |
| --- | --- |
| 11:00 | What am I avoiding right now? |
| 13:30 | Judging only by my behaviour today, what would a stranger say I want? |
| 15:15 | Is this moving me toward the life I want or the one I dread? |
| 17:00 | What important thing am I pretending is not important? |
| 19:30 | Today, what did I do to protect my image rather than to get what I want? |
| 21:00 | When did I feel most alive today? Most dead? |

The point is not the answers. The point is catching yourself **mid-behaviour**, when you can still see what you're doing. Answering these at the end of the day from memory does not work — you'll edit yourself.

Three more to carry around, no schedule:

- What changes if I stop needing people to see me as ______?
- Where am I trading aliveness for safety?
- What is the smallest version of my future self I could be tomorrow?

---

## Evening — Synthesis (45–60 min)

Read back everything you wrote today, then:

1. **Integration, 4 questions.** Two of them ask for exactly one sentence. Hold yourself to one sentence. If it takes a paragraph, you haven't found it yet.
2. **Goals, 3 questions.** Treat them as lenses, not finish lines — they exist to change what you notice tomorrow, not to be achieved.
3. **Fill the six components.** Anti-vision, Vision, 1-year goal, 1-month project, Daily levers, Constraints. Every one gets something, even rough.
4. Open the **Game board** tab. All six should be filled.
5. Press **Export Markdown**. Save the file somewhere you'll find it.

---

## The week after

This is where it usually dies, so:

- Put the six components somewhere you physically see them. Print them, or make the export your desktop wallpaper text, or pin the note.
- Do the **daily levers** — 2–3 tasks — before anything else, every day.
- **Re-read the anti-vision on the days you don't feel like it.** That is the entire job of the anti-vision. It is not motivational; it's the thing you're running from.
- Re-run this whole protocol in 3 months. The answers will have moved.

---

## Honest expectations

- One day does not fix a life. One day can produce **clarity**, and clarity is what makes the next six months not get wasted.
- Some of these questions land hard. That's the mechanism — tension builds, then things shift. But if the day surfaces something genuinely heavy, stop and talk to a professional. This is a worksheet, not treatment.
- If nothing lands, you may simply be in a chapter where the question isn't live yet. Put it down. Come back.
