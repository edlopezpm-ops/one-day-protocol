# Phone reminders

Part of [[Part 2 — Throughout the Day - Pattern Interruption]].

- [ ] **11:00** — What am I avoiding right now?
- [ ] **13:30** — Judging only by my behaviour today, what would a stranger say I want?
- [ ] **15:15** — Is this moving me toward the life I want or the one I dread?
- [ ] **17:00** — What important thing am I pretending is not important?
- [ ] **19:30** — Today, what did I do to protect my image rather than to get what I want?
- [ ] **21:00** — When did I feel most alive today? Most dead?
