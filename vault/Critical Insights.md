# Critical Insights

- **Change is sudden, but only after tension builds** — Dissonance leads to uncertainty, uncertainty leads to discovery.
- **Clarity compresses time, hours do not** — You cannot make six years of progress in six months without knowing exactly what you are aiming at.
- **This takes one full day of focused work** — Half-doing it produces half-answers.
- **It lands differently depending on your chapter** — Timing is part of the result. The same questions hit harder at some points in life than others.
- **Questioning is thinking** — Most of what passes for thinking is rehearsing things you already believe.
- **Your goals build your reality** — They decide what you notice and what you treat as valuable.
