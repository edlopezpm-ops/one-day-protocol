# Carry-around questions

No schedule. Keep these in your pocket and return to them whenever the day goes quiet.

Part of [[Part 2 — Throughout the Day - Pattern Interruption]].

- [ ] What changes if I stop needing people to see me as ______?
	- Fill the blank with the label you work hardest to maintain. Most of what that identity costs you stays invisible until you price it.
- [ ] Where am I trading aliveness for safety?
	- Safety is not the enemy; unexamined safety is. Find the specific trade you keep making without deciding to.
- [ ] What is the smallest version of my future self I could be tomorrow?
	- Not a transformation — the smallest recognisable version. One hour, one decision, one different answer.
