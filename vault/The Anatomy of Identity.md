# The Anatomy of Identity

Part of [[Key Psychological Principles]].

- **The identity loop** — Goal → perception → attention → action → feedback → automatic → "who you are" → defended → generates new goals.
	- The loop is self-sealing: goals decide what you notice, what you notice confirms the goal, and confirmation hardens into identity, which then generates more goals of the same kind. Breaking it means changing the goal at the top, not the behaviour at the bottom.
- **It started before you were consulted** — Most of the loop was installed in childhood by environment and conditioning.
	- You adopted a strategy that made one specific childhood environment survivable. It worked — that is precisely why it stayed. The only live question is whether that is still the environment you are in.
- **After survival comes ideology** — Once the body is safe, you defend beliefs as if they were food and shelter.
	- Being wrong starts to feel like dying. That is why people will burn a friendship rather than update a position, and why identity-level change feels genuinely dangerous rather than merely difficult.
