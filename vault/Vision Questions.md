# Vision Questions

Three questions about the life you are aiming at. Keep the same level of detail you used for the anti-vision — an ordinary Tuesday, not a highlight reel.

Part of [[Part 1 — Morning - Psychological Excavation]].

- [ ] Three years out, best realistic case. Describe an ordinary Tuesday in detail.
	- Best realistic case, not fantasy. Detail is what makes it usable: the hour you wake, the work in front of you, who you eat with, what you are no longer worried about.
- [ ] What would you have to believe about yourself for that life to feel normal?
	- Beliefs are load-bearing. That life stays out of reach for as long as you hold beliefs that make it absurd for you, specifically, to have it.
- [ ] If you were already that person, what would you do this week?
	- This converts the vision into something testable within seven days. Small enough to actually do, and unmistakably that person's move.
