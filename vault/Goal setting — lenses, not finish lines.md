# Goal setting — lenses, not finish lines

These exist to change what you notice tomorrow, not to be crossed off. A goal you never reach can still have done its job if it aimed your attention correctly for a year.

Part of [[Part 3 — Evening - Synthesis]].

- [ ] 1 year: what would have to be true for you to know the pattern is broken?
	- Pick evidence, not a feeling. Something an outside observer could confirm twelve months from now.
- [ ] 1 month: what must be true this month to keep the 1-year goal possible?
	- This is the constraint that keeps the year honest — what has to be true in 30 days for the 1-year answer to still be on the table.
- [ ] Daily: which 2–3 actions would your future self simply do, without debate?
	- Two or three, not ten. They have to be doable on a bad day, or they are not levers.
