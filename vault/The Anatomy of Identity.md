# The Anatomy of Identity

Part of [[Key Psychological Principles]].

- **The identity loop** — Set a goal, perceive reality through it, notice what is relevant, act and get feedback, repeat until automatic, it becomes "who you are", you defend it, and it starts generating your next goals.
- **It started before you were consulted** — Most of the loop was installed in childhood by environment and conditioning.
- **After survival comes ideology** — Once the body is safe, you begin defending beliefs as if they were food and shelter.
