# Identity First, Actions Second

Part of [[Key Psychological Principles]].

- **Effort is a symptom, not a strategy** — People who look like they are grinding are usually just living a life that fits them.
	- If a behaviour takes constant willpower, it has not been absorbed yet. Sustainable output looks boring from the inside: the person doing it is not overcoming anything, they are just living.
- **Adopt the lifestyle before the outcome** — Live as the person who already has the result.
	- Waiting for the result to justify the identity gets the order backwards. The habits, standards, hours and company of the person you want to be are the input, not the reward for arriving.
- **Misfit habits should start to repel you** — Old habits stop feeling tempting and start feeling wrong.
	- This is the reliable signal that identity actually moved. You are not white-knuckling past the old habit — it stopped reading as a reward. If it still tempts you, the shift has not happened yet.
