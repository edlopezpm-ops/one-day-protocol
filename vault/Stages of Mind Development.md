# Stages of Mind Development

Part of [[Key Psychological Principles]].

- **Most people sit between self-aware and construct-aware** — Roughly stages 4 to 8 in adult-development models.
	- Translated: aware that you have conditioning, not yet fluent at rewriting it. You can see the box. You are still furnishing it.
- **Development is sequential** — You cannot skip a stage. You can only move through the next one.
	- You can borrow the conclusions of a later stage, but they collapse the first time they are tested, because you did not do the work that made them yours.
- **The upper stages question inherited beliefs** — You stop borrowing principles and start writing your own.
	- The shift is from "what should I believe?" to "which of my beliefs did I actually choose?" Principles you wrote yourself hold under pressure. Inherited ones do not.
