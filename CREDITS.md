## Dan Koe — the original work

The article **"How to fix your entire life in 1 day"** and the underlying framework

and the "turn your life into a game" framing) are Dan Koe's.

- Website: <https://thedankoe.com/>

Read the original. This repository is a study aid, not a replacement for it.

## @MindBranches — the mind map

The **structure** used here — the five branches, the order they run in, and the way the
protocol is split into morning / through-the-day / evening — follows the mind-map summary
published by @MindBranches:

- <https://x.com/MindBranches/status/2078988089393901842>

That summary is what made the article usable as a tool. The interactive map, the Obsidian
canvas, and the two colour palettes in this repo are all built on that organisation.

## What this repository actually contains

Everything here was written from scratch for this project:

- the prose in `data/model.json` — a paraphrase, in different words, of the ideas above;
- the interactive map and workbook in `index.html`;
- the Obsidian vault, canvas, and worksheet generated by `tools/build.mjs`;
- the SOPs in `docs/`.

**It does not contain:** the text of Dan Koe's article, the @MindBranches image files, or
any PDF, screenshot, or copy of either. Nothing from the original works is redistributed
here. The images in `docs/img/` are screenshots of this repository's own interface.
