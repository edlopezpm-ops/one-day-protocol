# Six Components to Document

This is the deliverable of the whole day. Every slot gets something, even a rough draft. Keep it where you will physically see it.

Part of [[Implementation - Turn Life Into a Game]].

- **Anti-vision** — The life you refuse to end up in. *(What is at stake if you lose.)*
	- Write it as a scene, not a fear. You will re-read this on the days you do not feel like doing the work — that is its only job.
- **Vision** — The life you are building toward. *(The win condition.)*
	- A direction, not a destination. Concrete enough that you would recognise it if you arrived.
- **1-year goal** — What is true twelve months from now. *(The main mission.)*
	- One sentence, verifiable. If you could argue about whether you hit it, it is still too vague.
- **1-month project** — The skill to acquire or the thing to build. *(The boss fight, where the XP comes from.)*
	- Something that ships or is learned inside 30 days. It should scare you slightly and produce evidence either way.
- **Daily levers** — The 2–3 tasks that actually move it. *(Daily quests.)*
	- Two or three tasks, done before anything else. These are the only things that must happen for a day to count.
- **Constraints** — What you will not sacrifice to get it. *(The rules that force creativity.)*
	- Health, relationships, ethics, sleep — whatever you will not trade. Constraints are not obstacles; they force the creative path.
