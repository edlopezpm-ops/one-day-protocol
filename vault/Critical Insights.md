# Critical Insights

*Things worth knowing before you expect a result.*

- **Change is sudden, but only after tension builds** — Dissonance leads to uncertainty, uncertainty leads to discovery.
	- Nothing changes, nothing changes, then everything does. The plateau is the tension accumulating, and it is exactly where most people quit.
- **Clarity compresses time, hours do not** — You cannot make six years of progress in six months without knowing what you are aiming at.
	- Effort spent in the wrong direction is still effort. Clarity is what makes the same number of hours count for more.
- **This takes one full day of focused work** — Half-doing it produces half-answers.
	- Two hours gives you back the answers you already had. The useful material only shows up once you have exhausted the rehearsed ones.
- **It lands differently depending on your chapter** — Timing is part of the result.
	- If the questions do not land, the timing may be wrong rather than you. Put it down and come back in a few months.
- **Questioning is thinking** — Most of what passes for thinking is rehearsing things you already believe.
	- Rehearsing a position feels like thinking and produces nothing new. A real question is one you do not already have the answer to.
- **Your goals build your reality** — They decide what you notice and what you treat as valuable.
	- Change the goal and the same room contains different information. This is why a genuine shift in perspective feels like the world changed rather than you.
