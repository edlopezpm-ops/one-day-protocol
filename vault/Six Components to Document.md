# Six Components to Document

Part of [[Implementation - Turn Life Into a Game]].

- **Anti-vision** — The life you refuse to end up in. *(What is at stake if you lose.)*
- **Vision** — The life you are building toward. *(The win condition.)*
- **1-year goal** — What is true twelve months from now. *(The main mission.)*
- **1-month project** — The skill to acquire or the thing to build. *(The boss fight, where the XP comes from.)*
- **Daily levers** — The 2–3 tasks that actually move it. *(Daily quests.)*
- **Constraints** — What you will not sacrifice to get it. *(The rules that force creativity.)*
