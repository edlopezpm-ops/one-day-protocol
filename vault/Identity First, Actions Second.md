# Identity First, Actions Second

Part of [[Key Psychological Principles]].

- **Effort is a symptom, not a strategy** — People who look like they are grinding are usually just living a life that fits them.
- **Adopt the lifestyle before the outcome** — Live as the person who already has the result. The result is what that life produces.
- **Misfit habits should start to repel you** — Once identity actually shifts, old habits stop feeling tempting and start feeling wrong.
