# Game Mechanics

The six components map onto the parts of any decent game. This is not decoration — it is why the structure holds attention when a to-do list does not.

Part of [[Implementation - Turn Life Into a Game]].

- **Vision = the win condition**
	- Know what winning looks like, or every choice becomes a coin flip.
- **Anti-vision = what is at stake if you lose**
	- Stakes are what make a game real. No downside, no urgency.
- **1-year goal = the main mission**
	- The mission is what gives this month its meaning. Without it, the month is busywork.
- **1-month project = the boss fight**
	- Where skill actually increases. Boss fights, not grinding.
- **Daily levers = daily quests**
	- Small, repeatable, done every day. This is where the hours actually go.
- **Constraints = the rules that force creativity**
	- Limits force invention. A game with no rules is not a game.
